Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Xeven ("we", "us") collects when you visit our site or buy our products, and how we use and protect it.

1Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the store:

  • Order details: your name, email address, and billing details that you enter at checkout.
  • Messages: anything you send us when you contact support.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser, device type, and pages visited, collected through standard web server logs and the third-party services described below.

2How we use your information

We use your information to deliver your purchase, send order and download emails, answer support requests, process refunds, prevent fraud and misuse, meet legal obligations, and improve the site. We do not sell your personal information.

3Payments

Payments are handled by a third-party checkout and payment provider. Your card details go directly to that provider. We do not see or store your full card number.

4Cookies and third-party services

Our site loads fonts, icons, and scripts from third-party services such as Google Fonts and content delivery networks. These services receive your IP address when your browser requests those files. Our checkout provider may also set cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features may stop working.

5Sharing your information

We share personal information only with service providers that help us operate, such as our payment and checkout provider and email or file delivery tools, and when required by law or to protect our rights. These providers may only use your information to perform services for us.

6Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as needed to fulfil orders, provide support, handle refunds, and meet tax, accounting, and legal requirements. After that, we delete or anonymize it.

7Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict certain uses, and to withdraw consent. US state residents, such as those in California, may also have rights to know about and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising. To make a request, email us at the address below. We may need to verify your identity first.

9International processing

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where required, we take steps to make sure your information stays protected.

10Children

Our products are meant for adults running or starting an online business. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

12Contact us

Questions or requests about privacy? Email support@xeven.store.
